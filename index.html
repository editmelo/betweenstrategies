The Between

The real work lives in the space between.

Between extraction and regeneration there is a liminal space — not a destination, but a direction. It’s where people stop waiting to be fixed and start finding each other.

Between Strategies works in that space. We don’t arrive with answers. We design the conditions — the relationships, the conversations, the structures — that let communities and organizations move on their own terms.

“The work isn’t about fixing communities. It’s about building the space where communities can move.”

Frameworks & Language

The Between Strategies Lexicon

Names matter. These are the frameworks we use with integrity — big vision held in practical language that names things clearly.

01

The Between

The liminal space between extraction and regeneration — where transformation is possible. Not a destination but a direction, held in practice and relationship.

02

PIVOT Approach

A facilitation framework for navigating change: Pause, Investigate, Vision, Organize, Transform. Used in community spaces and organizational transitions.

03

Business of the Network

A structured conversation methodology for surfacing questions, building collective intelligence, and moving from insight to action within networks.

04

Network Weaving

The practice of building connections between people and communities — mapping relationships, bridging gaps, and strengthening the tissue of collective power.

05

Transformation Architecture

Designing the structural conditions for change. Systems thinking applied to community and organizational development with roots in lived experience.

06

Community Healing

Centering the relational dimensions of community change. Not extractive, not performative — rooted in place, people, and the long work of repair.

About the Founder

Wyndi Moore

Transformation Architect · Network Weaver · Community Healer

Wyndi founded Between Strategies on a simple conviction: communities already hold what they need to move. What’s often missing are the conditions — the trust, the connective tissue, the room to imagine together.

Her work lives at the intersection of network weaving, facilitation, and systems design, rooted in place and in lived experience. Whether she’s convening neighbors in Ward 8 or guiding an organization through transition, the relationship always comes before the work.
